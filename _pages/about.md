I’m interested in AI, machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and information retrieval. I’ve worked on projects involving computer vision and data processing, focusing on practical, reproducible approaches to real-world problems.

I’m particularly interested in applying machine learning in areas like healthcare, vision-based monitoring, and intelligent information systems, with an emphasis on building reliable and useful systems.
